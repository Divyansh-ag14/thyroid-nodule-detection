    Limitations

    What these results do not show

    • The model assesses a nodule that is already in the image; it does not find nodules.
    • Accuracy drops on images from a different source, and probabilities need re-calibrating for a new hospital's patient mix.
    • Small nodules are missed more often than larger ones.
    • Neither dataset has patient IDs, so separate images of the same patient cannot be fully ruled out.
    • Research prototype: not a medical device and not for clinical decisions.
