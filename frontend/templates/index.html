How to read this result
  • Low suspicion: below the rule-out cut-off. About 9 in 10 cancers in testing scored above it.
  • Indeterminate: the model is not confident either way. Use specialist review and TI-RADS scoring.
  • High suspicion: above the rule-in cut-off. About 9 in 10 benign nodules in testing scored below it.
  • The evidence map shows where the model looked, not a nodule outline. The model does not detect nodules and assumes one is present.
  • Probabilities were calibrated on one dataset and may need adjusting for a different hospital's patient mix.
